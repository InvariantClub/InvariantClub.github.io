Very excited to announce our first Nix workshop!

Join us for a full day of hands-on hacking on Nix; covering:

- Getting started,
- Installing packages,
- Development environments,
- Deployment,
- Testing,
- and more!

### Essentials: Where/When/How much

- *When*: 29th of September
- *Cost*: £320.00 + VAT
- *Tickets*: [Nix Workshop ∘ Edinburgh ∘ September 2026](https://luma.com/k65ttfw3)
- *Where*: [CodeBase](https://maps.app.goo.gl/zb6wzxkEbsALbaEA8), Edinburgh.


### Why a Nix workshop?

- Nix is amazing!
- Getting started alone is very challenging
- Learn in a friendly small-group environment
- Hands on experience
- BYO ecosystems, get specific advice

### Who should attend?

People who are:

- _Willing to hack_: This is a hands-on workshop; you will be expected to
  have your laptop, to be able to install things, and to be programming
  activities as we go.

- _Excited to learn_: Our goal will be to gain a deep and functional
  understanding of Nix and how to use it. We don't

#### Which programming languages will you support?

Our working examples will be in `Python`, `JavaScript`, and `Rust`, but there
will be time to explore other ecosystems through-out the day. The general
principles we will learn will be relevant to all languages and ecosystems, so
you should find it valuable even if your day-to-day work is in a different
space.


#### Will I need access to an AI agent?

No; you can definitely use one if you wish, but the aim will be to be learning
details that will help you either do further work yourself, or _guide_ agents
towards the right implementation approach.

### Agenda

We'll cover the following areas:

- Nix - What's it useful for?
- Reproducible development environments
- Packaging for deployment
- Structuring your Nix codebase
- Nixing and AI
- What to do when things go wrong and how to fix them
- Next steps

This will be a hands-on workshop; a laptop is required; you will leave with
real-world experience using Nix in your projects, for wildly increased joy of
development!
